Probability and life sciences

I develop and study probabilistic models for the life sciences.

I am a research director at INRAE, in the SOLsTIS team of the MIA-Paris-Saclay laboratory.

My work focuses in particular on the genetic evolution of biparental populations and on the combination of datasets of different qualities, with applications to ecology and the environment.

My habilitation thesis (HDR) is available online.

Recent work

My recent work has dealt with the distribution of the probability that a gene originates from a given ancestor in a biparental population with selection, and with the impact of a periodic environment on the mutation–selection balance.

Teaching

I coordinate the second-year Master’s programme Mathematics for Life Sciences and its student projects. I contribute to the course “Modèles aléatoires en écologie et en évolution” within the M2 EvoGEM programme (Biodiversity, Ecology and Evolution — BEE).

Background

I did my PhD at the Centre de Mathématiques Appliquées of École Polytechnique, under the supervision of Sylvie Méléard. I was then a Hadamard Lecturer, and later an associate professor (maîtresse de conférences) at the Laboratoire de Mathématiques d’Orsay and at the IUT de Sceaux. From 2023 to 2026, I held an INRAE junior professor chair, during which I taught probability in the first-year Master’s programme in Fundamental Mathematics at Université Paris-Saclay.
